Pricing FAQ

Common questions about our plans and payment

Can I pay in EMI installments? ▼
Yes, all three plans support easy monthly EMI with zero extra charges. You can split your fees into 2, 3, or 6 months. Contact our team to set up a payment plan that works for you.
What is included in the job guarantee? ▼
The Premium plan includes a 100% job guarantee — we work with you until you are placed. This includes resume building, mock interviews, referrals to our 40+ hiring partners, and direct job interview opportunities.
Can I upgrade my plan later? ▼
Yes. You can upgrade from Basic to Standard or Standard to Premium anytime by paying the difference. Just speak to our counselor and we will adjust your plan accordingly.
Is there a scholarship or discount available? ▼
Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships up to 50% off for deserving students. Early enrollment discounts of 10% are also available for the first 10 students of each new batch.
Are there any hidden fees or registration charges? ▼
No hidden charges at all. The monthly price you see is all you pay. Study materials, lab access, and certification fees are included in every plan.

Not sure which plan is right for you?

Talk to our counselor for free. We'll help you pick the best course and plan based on your goal and budget.
