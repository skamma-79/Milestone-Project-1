Our Story

Happy Coding was founded with one mission — to provide quality, affordable computer education to the students of Jaipur. What started as a small classroom with 10 students has grown into a full-fledged institute with 500+ alumni working across India's top companies.

We believe that every student deserves access to real-world skills. That's why our courses are practical-first — you build real projects, work with real tools, and leave with a portfolio that speaks for itself.

From Web Design to Tally GST, from Digital Marketing to MS Office — we cover the skills that employers actually hire for.
