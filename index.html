F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I enroll in a course? ▼
You can enroll by visiting our campus or registering online through our website. Our team will guide you through the process.
What is the duration of the courses? ▼
Course duration varies from 3 months to 12 months depending on the program. We offer both short-term certification courses and comprehensive diploma programs.
Do you provide placement assistance? ▼
Yes, we provide 100% placement assistance to all our students. We have tie-ups with top companies and organize campus interviews regularly.
Can I pay the fees in installments? ▼
Yes, we offer flexible payment options. You can pay in monthly installments without any extra charges. Contact our team for customized plans.
Are the certificates recognized? ▼
All our certificates are industry-recognized and valid across India. We are a registered training institute with government accreditation.
Do you offer online classes? ▼
Yes, we offer both online and offline classes. Our online classes are live and interactive, with recorded sessions available for revision.
What if I miss a class? ▼
No worries! All classes are recorded and available in your student portal. You can also attend backup sessions or get personal doubt clearing.
Is there any scholarship available? ▼
Yes, we offer merit-based scholarships up to 50% off for deserving students. We also have special discounts for early enrollments.
